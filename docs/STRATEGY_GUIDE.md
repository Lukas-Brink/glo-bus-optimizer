# Glo-Bus Grand Champion Strategic Playbook

This strategy guide outlines the core mechanics and empirical winning strategies for the McGraw-Hill **GLO-BUS** business simulation (Action Cameras & UAV Drones).

---

## 1. The Five Scoring Pillars & Scoring Standards
In GLO-BUS, your performance is graded against both **Investor Expectations** and **Best-in-Industry**:
1. **Earnings Per Share (EPS)** (typically 20-25% weight)
2. **Return on Equity (ROE)** (typically 20-25% weight)
3. **Stock Price** (typically 20-25% weight)
4. **Credit Rating** (typically 15-20% weight - Target A or A+)
5. **Image Rating** (typically 15-20% weight - Target 75+)

---

## 2. The Golden Rules of Winning Glo-Bus

### 🏆 Rule #1: Max Out R&D Expenditure Early
- On the **Product Design** screen, maximize your cumulative R&D budget (up to the $50,000k lifetime cap) as early in the game as cash allows (usually Years 6–8).
- **Why**: Higher cumulative R&D unlocks **permanent cumulative manufacturing cost discounts per unit** and boosts your P/Q star rating without adding to ongoing material costs!

### 💰 Rule #2: Repurchase the Maximum 5% of Stock Every Round
- Under **Finance & Cash Flow**, GLO-BUS allows companies to repurchase up to **5% of outstanding shares** per year.
- **Why**: EPS is $\frac{\text{Net Profit}}{\text{Shares Outstanding}}$. When you shrink the denominator by 5% year after year, your EPS and ROE compound at an extraordinary rate. Teams that fail to buy back stock almost never reach #1 globally.

### 🛡️ Rule #3: Never Let Ending Cash Turn Negative (Overdraft Trap)
- If your projected **Ending Cash Balance** drops below \$0, the simulation forces an **emergency 1-year overdraft loan** at penal interest rates (often 12% to 15%+).
- This severely degrades your **Credit Rating** and wipes out future earnings.
- **Always maintain a safe cash cushion of \$2,500k to \$5,000k** to buffer against competitor price cuts that could reduce your final sales volume.

### 👥 Rule #4: Incentive Pay Beats High Base Wages
- On **Workforce Compensation & Assembly**, shift compensation toward **assembly incentives per defect-free unit** and **fringe benefits** rather than just higher base wages.
- Workers assemble more units per hour, and defect rates drop dramatically, lowering warranty claims and rework costs.
- Turn on **Robotics Upgrades** as soon as cash allows—they increase workstation assembly capacity by 25%.

### 📈 Rule #5: Overtime Before Capital Expansion
- Before spending capital to purchase expensive new workstations, utilize up to **15%–20% overtime**.
- Overtime is flexible and carries no long-term depreciation burden when demand fluctuations occur.

## 3. Decision Screen Breakdown

| Decision Page | Top Priority Levers | Key Watch-outs |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product Design** | Cumulative R&D, sensor, LCD, extra battery | Don't over-specify niche features with high unit costs and low P/Q impact |
| **Marketing** | Regional pricing, search engine ads, retailer support | Avoid advertising past diminishing returns (logarithmic curve) |
| **Assembly / Workforce** | Assembly incentive, training budget, robotics | Watch workstation utilization; keep overtime &le; 20% |
| **CSR & Citizenship** | Cafeteria & childcare, safety improvements | Avoid overspending on multiple redundant green initiatives unless chasing Image points |
| **Finance & Cash Flow** | 5% stock buyback, debt retirement, modest dividends | Ensure Ending Cash stays &ge; $3,000k; protect Credit Rating &ge; A- |
